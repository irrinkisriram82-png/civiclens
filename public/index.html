About CivicLens

CivicLens helps residents report civic problems such as potholes, garbage, water leaks and broken streetlights, and makes it public whether they get fixed. It is a hackathon prototype built for the IDEA//FORGE Online Hackathon (Track 1).

How AI is used

  • Reads the photo and the description (Telugu, Hindi, English and more) to identify the issue type.
  • Rates damage size and safety risk, and writes a short English summary with the visible evidence.
  • Compares a "before" and an "after" photo to check whether a repair was really done.
  • Rejects photos that are not civic issues.

What is not AI

The AI only supplies ratings. The 0 to 100 priority score, duplicate and photo-reuse checks, routing, escalation by service target, permissions and the audit log are ordinary, inspectable code, so the same inputs always give the same result.

Human oversight

AI output is advisory. Citizens can correct the category before submitting, and officers can override category, status and assignment. Every privileged action is recorded in an audit log.

Privacy note

Photos and text are sent to the AI provider for analysis. Please do not upload photos of people or private property.

Limitations

Sending complaints to authorities is simulated in this prototype. Data is stored in a simple file store, which would move to a database in production.
